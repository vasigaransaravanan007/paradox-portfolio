Privacy Policy — Paradox Agency

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Paradox Agency (“we,” “our,” “us”) operates www.paradoxagency.in and runs marketing campaigns, including lead generation forms on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, ads, or lead forms, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address
  • Business Information: Business type/industry, marketing budget range, and other details you provide through our qualifying questions

2. How We Collect Information

  • Directly from you: When you fill out an Instant Form on Facebook/Instagram, contact us via WhatsApp, email, or phone, or submit information through our website

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and assess if our services fit your business needs
  • Contact you via phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding our services
  • Improve our marketing campaigns and measure ad performance
  • Send you relevant updates about our services (you can opt out anytime)
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third-party partners who help us operate our business (e.g., CRM tools, scheduling software like Calendly, communication platforms like WhatsApp Business)

  • Legal authorities: If required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law. If you no longer wish to be contacted, you may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you

To exercise these rights, contact us at saif@paradoxagency.in.
